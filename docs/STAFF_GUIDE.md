# QueueCare --- Staff Guide

One page for reception, one for doctors. Print the one you need and keep
it at your desk.

---

## Reception

**Sign in** at the QueueCare address your clinic gave you, with your
email and password. You land on the reception dashboard.

### Running the queue

Open **Live queue**. The big card at the top is **Now serving**; below it
is **Up next**.

| To... | Do this |
|---|---|
| Call the next patient | Press **Call next**, or the **N** key. Tell them their token number and room. |
| Call again when nobody comes | Press **Recall**. The arrival countdown starts again. |
| Mark someone who didn't come | Press **Skip**, then **Skip patient** to confirm. |
| Give a skipped patient another turn | Press **Requeue**. They go to the end of the line and keep their number. |
| Undo a mistake | Press **Undo** on the message that appears for a few seconds. |
| Stop new patients joining (break, closing) | Press **Pause queue**. Waiting patients keep their place, and Call next is off until you press **Resume**. |

The bar on the Now serving card counts down the time a called patient
has to arrive. In Up next, a wait time turns orange when someone has
waited longer than the clinic's target.

### Walk-in patients

Press **Register walk-in** (on a phone, the round **+** button), type the
patient's **first name or initials**, choose the service and confirm. Say
the token number out loud --- that's all they need.

### When things look odd

- **"…was just updated by someone else. The queue has been refreshed."**
  --- another receptionist called or changed that patient first. Check
  the refreshed screen and carry on.
- **A patient's phone looks out of date** --- it updates every 15
  seconds. Ask them to refresh the page.
- **A patient lost their link** --- look them up in **Queue history** by
  token number and call them by number.
- **The screen shows an error** --- refresh the page. If it keeps
  happening, note the time and the reference on the screen and tell your
  clinic admin.

### Privacy

Type a **first name or initials only**. Never type phone numbers, ages,
symptoms or anything medical. Names are deleted automatically after the
clinic's retention period.

---

## Doctors

**Sign in** with your email and password. You land on your dashboard,
which shows your current patient and who is next.

### Seeing patients

1. Reception calls the patient. They appear on your screen as called.
2. When they come in, press **Start** (Start consultation).
3. When you're finished, press **Complete** (Complete consultation). The
   patient's phone shows their visit is done.

You can have **one** patient in consultation at a time; complete the
current one before starting the next. **My queue** shows who is waiting
for your services.

### Breaks and delays

Open **Availability** and choose **Available**, **On break** or
**Off duty**. If you're running late, add how many minutes. Reception
sees your status, and waiting patients see that the doctors are on a
break or running late --- they never see your name.

Set yourself back to **Available** when you return.

### What QueueCare shows you

Only the patient's name, token number and service. QueueCare is not a
medical record: don't keep notes in it.

---

**Problems?** Tell your clinic admin, with the time it happened and any
reference number on the screen.
